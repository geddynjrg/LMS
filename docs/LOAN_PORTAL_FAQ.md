# Uwezo Fund Loans Portal — How-To FAQ

Step-by-step guide for **groups applying for loans** on the Uwezo Fund applicant portal.

| Environment | URL |
|-------------|-----|
| **Staging** | https://loans-staging.uwezo.go.ke |
| **Production** | https://loans.uwezo.go.ke |

This portal is separate from the **LMS** (`lms.uwezo.go.ke`), which constituency and HQ staff use to review your application after you submit.

For staff workflows after submission, see [HOW_TO.md](./HOW_TO.md) and [FAQ.md](./FAQ.md).

---

## Before you start

### Who can apply?

Self-help groups that meet Uwezo Fund eligibility:

- Registered with the Department of Social Services, Cooperatives, or Registrar of Societies (valid certificate)
- **10–15 members** as guided by the registering body
- Membership composition:
  - **Youth groups:** ≥ 70% youth (18–35), leadership 100% youth
  - **Women’s groups:** ≥ 70% women (18+), leadership 100% women
  - **PWD groups:** ≥ 70% persons with disabilities, leadership 100% PWD
- Operates table banking or similar group savings
- Holds a **bank or SACCO account** in the group’s name

### What documents should I prepare?

| Document | When needed |
|----------|-------------|
| Group registration certificate | Step 1 — Group data |
| Signed list of members (on certificate) | Step 1 |
| Minutes from **at least 3 recent meetings** | Step 1 |
| Group credit history (table banking ledger, discharge certificate, clearance letter, etc.) | Step 1 (if applicable) |
| National ID photo for **each member** | Step 2 — Members |
| PWD certificate (for members with disability) | Step 2 (if applicable) |

**File rules:** PDF or image (JPG, PNG, WebP). Maximum **30 MB** per file.

### What loan products can I apply for?

| Product | Who | Amount range | Repayment |
|---------|-----|--------------|-----------|
| **Wezesha** | First-time borrowers | KES 50,000 – 100,000 | Up to 24 months |
| **Endeleza** | Repeat borrowers | Up to KES 500,000 (scaled from prior loan) | Up to 24 months |

A **1% administrative fee** is deducted upfront. The constituency officer may approve a different final amount during review.

## Account and login

### How do I create an account?

1. Open https://loans-staging.uwezo.go.ke (or production URL).
2. Click **Sign up** (or **Register through eCitizen** if using government SSO).
3. Enter email and password (with confirmation), or complete eCitizen registration.
4. After signup you are logged in automatically.

### How do I log in?

You can log in using:

- **Email and password**
- **Phone number + SMS OTP** (6-digit code sent to your phone)
- **eCitizen** (government single sign-on)

Forgot password? Use **Reset** on the login page and check your email for instructions.

### Is my progress saved?

Yes. The application is **saved at each step**. Log out and return later — use **Continue filling application** on your dashboard to resume where you left off.

## Starting an application

### How do I start a new application?

1. Log in to your account.
2. From the dashboard, click **Start New Application** (or use the button on the empty-state card).
3. Read **What you need to know before applying** on the instructions page.
4. Click **Begin filling the form** — you are taken to **Step 1: Group data**.

Each new application gets a unique **application ID** (shown at the top of every step).

### How many applications can I have?

Your dashboard lists all applications under **My Applications**. You can start a new application while another is in progress, but only complete applications that meet eligibility rules will be processed.

## The 7 application steps

A progress stepper at the top shows where you are. Complete steps in order.

```
1. Group data
2. Group members
3. Loan conditions
4. Proposed business
5. Members confirmation (OTP)
6. Confirmations (declarations)
7. Final review → Submit
```

Use **Back** and **Next** at the bottom of each step. **Next** only advances when required fields on that step are valid.

---

## Step 1 — Group data

### What do I fill in?

**Group basics**

- Group name (as on registration certificate)
- Registration number
- Date formed
- Upload: registration certificate, signed member list, meeting minutes (minimum one file; upload minutes from at least 3 meetings)

**Group purpose and activities**

- Purpose of the group (select all that apply)
- Key activities (e.g. welfare, income generating, table banking, value chain sectors)
- If you select **Table banking**, also enter:
  - Years of table banking practice
  - Monthly share contribution
  - Total amount loaned out
  - Total revolving fund
- If you select **Value chain**, choose the value chain category
- If you select **Other**, describe the activity
- Optional: upload credit history documents

**Location**

- County, constituency, and ward of operation
- Meeting point / physical location / village
- Nearest landmark (school, mosque, church, etc.)

### How do I save and continue?

Click **Next**. Data is saved to your application. You can return later from the dashboard.

---

## Step 2 — Group members

### How many members must I add?

The group should have **10–15 members** to be eligible. Add every member who will be part of this loan application.

### What details does each member need?

| Field | Required |
|-------|----------|
| Full name | Yes |
| National ID number | Yes |
| Gender | Yes |
| Phone number | Yes (unique per member) |
| Position (Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, Member, etc.) | Yes |
| Photo of ID | Yes |
| Economic activity (outside group) | Optional |
| PWD certificate + PWD number | If member has disability |

### What roles can only one person hold?

Only **one member** per application may hold each of:

- Chairperson / Chairman
- Secretary
- Treasurer

Duplicate phone numbers or national IDs across members are not allowed.

### How do I add members?

1. Select **Nature of the group** (Youth / Women / PWD).
2. Click **Add member**, fill the form, and save.
3. Repeat until all members are listed.
4. Click **Next** when finished.

---

## Step 3 — Loan conditions

### What do I enter?

- **Loan type:** Wezesha or Endeleza
- **Loan amount** (within product limits)
- **Proposed repayment period** (1–24 months)
- **Institution type:** Bank or SACCO
- Bank/SACCO name and branch
- **Group account number**

### What are the amount limits?

| Loan type | Minimum | Maximum |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Wezesha | KES 50,000 | KES 100,000 |
| Endeleza | — | KES 500,000 |

Enter amounts as numbers only (no commas). Use the slider or type the figure directly.

---

## Step 4 — Proposed business

### What do I fill in?

**Business information**

- Nature of business: **Start-up** or **Expansion**
- Business sector (from the provided list)
- Years the group has been in operation

**Business performance (monthly figures)**

- Average income per month
- Cost of goods/services per month
- Rent for business
- Salaries/wages
- Transport for business stock
- Optional: add custom expense lines

The form may show **net surplus/deficit** as you enter figures. Constituency reviewers use this data for loan recommendation.

---

## Step 5 — Members confirmation (OTP)

### Why must members confirm?

Every member must consent to the loan application. This step collects that consent before you submit.

### How does confirmation work?

1. For each member who has not yet responded, click **Send OTP**.
2. The member receives a **6-digit SMS code** on the phone number you entered in Step 2.
3. Enter the 6 digits in the field beside that member — verification runs **automatically** when 6 digits are entered.
4. On success, the member shows **Consented ✓**. If they decline, status shows **Declined ✗**.

### When can I click Next?

**Next** is enabled when at least **75% of listed members have responded** (confirmed or declined).

| Rule | Value |
|------|--------|
| Minimum responses to proceed from this step | 75% of total members (rounded up) |
| Counts as responded | Confirmed or declined |

You can close the browser and return later — progress is saved. Use **Resend OTP** after the countdown if a code expired (codes are valid for about 10 minutes).

### What if a member did not receive SMS?

1. Confirm the phone number in Step 2 is correct.
2. Wait for the resend timer, then click **Send OTP** again.
3. Ask the member to dictate the code to you so you can enter it on their behalf.

---

## Step 6 — Confirmations (declarations)

### What must I agree to?

Check all required boxes:

1. We confirm we are members of this group
2. We declare the information is true and authorize Uwezo Fund to verify it
3. We agree we are jointly and severally liable for repayment
4. We understand we are not eligible for additional loans until any default is cleared in full

Click **Next** after all boxes are ticked. You are taken to **Final review**.

---

## Step 7 — Final review and submit

### How do I submit?

1. Review all sections: loan details, members, group data, proposed business.
2. Click **Submit application**.
3. If anything is missing, a validation message lists errors — go back and fix them.
4. Confirm in the dialog: **Are you sure all information is correct?**
5. On success, you see the **Thank you** page.

### What checks block submission?

The system validates before submit, including:

- All required group documents and location fields
- At least one member with complete details
- **75% of members responded** to confirmation
- **75% of members consented** (confirmed) — not just declined
- No duplicate IDs or phone numbers
- Only one Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer
- Complete loan conditions and business figures
- All declaration checkboxes ticked

If submission fails, read the error list carefully — it tells you which step to fix.

### What happens after I submit?

1. You see: *“Thank you for your application!”*
2. You can **download** the application PDF or **email** it to yourself.
3. Go to **My applications** on the dashboard to track status.
4. Your application syncs to the constituency LMS for officer review.

## Tracking your application (dashboard)

### How do I see application status?

1. Log in and open **My Applications**.
2. Each card shows application ID, group name, loan amount, and **Status**.
3. Click **View application** (or **Continue filling application** if still a draft).

### What do the statuses mean?

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Unsubmitted / Draft** | Still filling the form — continue from your last step |
| **Submitted** | Received — waiting for constituency batch review |
| **Constituency review** | Your constituency officer is reviewing |
| **Approved by constituency** | Approved locally — proceeds toward HQ |
| **Being reviewed** | Further verification (committee / HQ pipeline) |
| **Approved** | Loan approved — await training/disbursement contact |
| **Training** | Group training scheduled or in progress |
| **Pending disbursement** | Awaiting cheque/disbursement |
| **Disbursed** | Funds sent to your group account |
| **Rejected** | Not approved — see rejection reason on the card |

The dashboard may show **What is being done in this step?** and **How long will it take?** for active statuses.

### How long does the process take?

- Member OTP confirmations: typically **1–3 days** (depends on members responding)
- After submit: constituency review — **a few days** to reach committee stage
- Full process to disbursement: often **about a month**, depending on phase batching and approvals

### What if my application is rejected?

The card shows **Rejection reason** and additional details. Address the issues and start a **new application** or resubmit per constituency guidance.

## After approval

### How do I download my training certificate?

When training has been completed and the certificate is ready (available from **four days after training start date**):

1. Open the application on your dashboard.
2. Click **Download training certificate**.

### How do I repay my loan online?

For **Approved** or **Disbursed** applications with an active loan:

1. Open the application card on the dashboard.
2. Click **Repay loan**.
3. Enter the amount to pay (up to outstanding balance).
4. Complete payment through **Pesaflow** in the secure checkout dialog.

Payments are processed via Pesaflow (not automatic M-Pesa STK push). If online repayment is unavailable, contact support or pay through official offline channels.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot log in

- Check email/password or request **Reset password**
- For phone login, use the correct **9-digit** Kenyan number and wait for the SMS code
- Try **eCitizen** login if you registered that way
- Clear browser cache or try another supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)

### My file upload failed

- Use **PDF, JPG, PNG, or WebP** only
- Keep each file under **30 MB**
- Ensure a stable internet connection and retry

### Next is disabled on Members confirmation

- At least **75% of members** must have **responded** (confirmed or declined)
- Follow up with members who show **SMS Not Sent** or **SMS Sent** but no response
- Re-send OTP after the countdown

### Submit fails — not enough members consented

Submission requires **75% confirmed**, not only 75% responded. Members who **declined** count as responded but not consented. Replace declined members or resolve objections before submitting.

### Duplicate phone number or national ID error

Each member must have a **unique** phone number and national ID. Edit the duplicate member in Step 2.

### Two leadership roles assigned to one position

Only one Chairperson, one Secretary, and one Treasurer per application. Reassign roles in Step 2.

### Application stuck on “Submitted” for a long time

Constituency officers batch applications into **phases** before committee review. No action is required on the portal unless the constituency contacts you. Use dashboard contact email/phone if you need help.

### Status on portal does not match what constituency told us

Status updates sync from the LMS periodically. Refresh the dashboard. If still wrong after 24 hours, contact constituency support with your **application ID**.

## Quick reference — applicant checklist

```
□ Account created and logged in
□ Step 1: Group certificate, minutes, location
□ Step 2: 10–15 members, unique IDs/phones, ID photos
□ Step 3: Loan type, amount, bank/SACCO account
□ Step 4: Business income and expenses
□ Step 5: ≥75% members responded via OTP
□ Step 6: All declarations checked
□ Step 7: Review and submit
□ ≥75% members consented (for successful submit)
□ Track status on dashboard
```

On the dashboard, use **Do you have questions?** for official contact email and phone.

When contacting support, provide:

- Your **application ID**
- Group name and constituency
- Screenshot of any error message
- Step number where you are stuck
